💡 How it works

  • Every login has one role. A role decides which menus and buttons the person sees.
  • View lets them look; Manage lets them add, change and delete.
  • Administrator always has full access and can't be changed, so you can never lock yourself out.
  • Everyone linked to an employee record also gets My Space (own attendance, leaves, payslips, assets).
  • Give a role to a person on Users & Logins.
